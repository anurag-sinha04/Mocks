Important Examination Guidelines

A Duration & Marking Scheme

  • Total duration of CLAT MOCK-1 is 120 minutes (2 Hours). When the timer reaches zero, your exam will be automatically submitted.
  • The paper consists of 120 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) across 5 sections: English Language (24), Current Affairs & GK (28), Legal Reasoning (32), Logical Reasoning (24), and Quantitative Techniques (12).
  • Correct Answer: +1.00 mark for each correct answer.
  • Incorrect Answer: -0.25 mark negative marking for each wrong answer.
  • Unattempted Question: 0 marks.

B Question Palette Status Legend

1 Not Visited: Not opened yet.
2 Not Answered: Visited, no option selected.
3 Answered: Saved for evaluation.
4 Marked for Review: Flagged, no answer chosen.
5 Answered & Marked for Review: Answer saved and flagged. (Evaluated in final score.)

C Answering & Navigation

  • Use Section Tabs at the top to jump between all 5 sections freely.
  • Use A- / A+ controls in the passage panel to adjust font size.
  • Use Save & Next, Mark for Review & Next, or Clear Response at any time.
